Phishing Shield AI — Real-Time Browser Protection
Tagline: Detect the deception. Understand the risk. Browse with confidence.

OVERVIEW
Phishing Shield AI is a lightweight, privacy-conscious Chrome extension that protects you against phishing emails, deceptive look-alike domains, credential harvesting login pages, and social engineering attacks before you share sensitive data.

Unlike legacy security tools that send your entire browsing history to centralized cloud servers, Phishing Shield AI operates a high-speed local defense pipeline. It analyzes active pages in under 15ms directly on your device using deterministic heuristics and Chrome's Built-in AI (Gemini Nano).

KEY FEATURES
• Look-Alike & Typosquatting Detection: Identifies deceptive domains that mimic major brands (e.g. PayPal, Chase, Microsoft, Google) using Levenshtein distance, keyword squatting, and subdomain masquerading.
• Unicode & Homoglyph Protection: Unmasks Cyrillic and Greek characters visually identical to Latin letters used to trick human eyes.
• Deceptive Link Mismatch Warning: Flags links where the visible anchor text displays a trusted service while the actual destination routes to a third-party server.
• Credential Harvesting Shield: Detects unexpected password inputs, one-time-password (OTP/2FA) interception fields, and credit card collection forms on unverified origins or plain HTTP.
• Psychological Urgency Detection: Evaluates high-pressure language ("account suspended", "24 hours to verify", "unpaid customs fee") common in social engineering scams.
• Isolated Shadow DOM Warning Banner: Automatically injects a high-visibility warning banner on dangerous websites without breaking site layout or normal functionality.
• Non-Destructive Link Outlining: Outlines suspicious embedded links with subtle red dashed borders and clear tooltip explanations.
• Plain-Language Explanations: Translates complex security cues into 2 clear sentences with one recommended action for non-technical users.
• Chrome Built-in AI (Gemini Nano): Uses the Chrome Prompt API for local social engineering pattern evaluation when supported.
• Privacy-Preserving PII Redaction: Automatically strips email addresses, phone numbers, credit card numbers, and session tokens before any optional external lookup.

HOW TO USE
1. Click "Add to Chrome" to install Phishing Shield AI.
2. The extension automatically evaluates the active tab as you browse.
3. The toolbar icon displays a live risk score (0 to 100) and color-coded status badge:
   - Green (0–29): Safe-looking (no threat indicators detected).
   - Amber (30–59): Suspicious (caution advised, check domain).
   - Red (60–100): Dangerous (active threat or impersonation detected).
4. Click the icon at any time to inspect the top reasons, suspicious links, and recommended actions.

PERMISSIONS JUSTIFICATION
• activeTab: Inspects the DOM structure and links of the active website only when opened or navigating.
• storage: Persists your preferences and local scan history in chrome.storage.local without transmitting data.
• scripting: Injects the warning banner on high-risk pages.
• sidePanel: Displays the detailed forensic inspection feed alongside your browsing window.
• Host Permissions (<all_urls>): Necessary to analyze URLs across arbitrary websites to identify zero-day phishing destinations.

PRIVACY COMMITMENT
Phishing Shield AI does not log your browsing history, sell user data, or store passwords. An optional "On-Device-Only Mode" is available in Settings to guarantee zero external network requests.
